What this can establish

Crossbar contacts trained the small model; stem contacts were held out from base fitting. Rotation and reflection make many records equivalent: only four response groups remain per family.

Probe updates may correct geometry shift as well as physics. This is inspected TRAIN development evidence, not proof of generalization or learned advantage. Qualification remains failed at 19.51% headroom versus 20% required. Benchmark tuning has stopped.

Each method is charged the same two probe episodes and decision: 726 controls, with matched resets. The fixed controls and oracle in the table are evaluator references, not extra learned methods.

A preserved chain of negative results

The original 1D pilot tied downstream actions. Routed PushT exposed reachability and model failures. Direct contact removed navigation but narrowly missed its qualification gate.
